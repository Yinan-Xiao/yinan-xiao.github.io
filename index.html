Yinan Xiao

Princeton / Adel Research Group | ynxiao@princeton.edu

I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate at Princeton University, working in the Adel Research Group at the School of Architecture with Arash Adel.

My research focuses on physics-grounded intelligent structural design, aiming to develop the next generation of computational methods and tools that transform how structures are conceived, explored, and realized. By integrating structural mechanics, geometric computation, and artificial intelligence (AI), I seek to enable designers to interactively generate, evaluate, and refine structurally informed and fabrication-aware solutions.

I hold a Dr.-Ing. (summa cum laude) from Technische Universität Braunschweig, with doctoral co-supervision at the Technical University of Munich. My doctoral research, supervised by Norman Hack and Pierluigi D’Acunto, connected structural design, graphic statics, and automated robotic fabrication for Injection 3D Concrete Printing. I previously worked at The University of Hong Kong and The Chinese University of Hong Kong, and hold an M.Sc. from Aalto University and a B.Arch. from Huazhong University of Science and Technology.
